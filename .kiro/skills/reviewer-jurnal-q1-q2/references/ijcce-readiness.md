## Editorial fit

- Connect the work to cognitive computing and a real engineering problem.
- Explain why the technical contribution is more than an application of standard classification.
- State device, runtime, biometric task, and evidence boundaries when edge deployment is central.
- Check the closest IJCCE and external literature rather than assuming that NAS + KD is novel.

IJCCE uses single-blind review and normally sends suitable work to at least two reviewers. The editor may desk-reject for scope mismatch, ethical issues, high similarity, insufficient originality, or flaws in research design/methods.

## Mandatory readiness gates

- Native editable source, simple single-column layout, no justified text or automatic hyphenation.
- Numbered sections and subsections with consistent internal references.
- Stand-alone factual abstract and accurate keywords.
- Reproducible methods and concise results.
- Discussion interprets significance instead of duplicating results.
- Complete title page and corresponding-author details.
- CRediT roles, funding-role statement, acknowledgements, and competing-interest document.
- Data/code availability statement consistent with biometric privacy and licenses.
- APA author–year citations; complete bidirectional citation/reference matching.
- Track Changes disabled in submission and supplementary files.

The public guide does not specify a manuscript page limit, word limit, numeric similarity cutoff, or AI-detector threshold. Treat any claimed cutoff as unsupported unless the journal communicates it directly.

## Tables and artwork

- Tables are editable, sequentially numbered, referenced, and free of vertical rules/shading.
- Table notes appear below the table and data are not needlessly duplicated in prose.
- Equations remain editable; variables and numbering follow the guide.
- Captions are supplied separately from figure pixels and define symbols/abbreviations.
- Each illustration is also uploaded as a separate high-resolution file.
- Raster requirements: at least 300 dpi photographs, 1000 dpi line art, and 500 dpi combination artwork.
- Color choices remain readable for people with color-vision deficiency.

Highlights are optional but encouraged: separate editable file, 3–5 bullets, maximum 85 characters including spaces. Graphical abstract is optional and follows separate size, licensing, and AI-tool rules.

## Ethics and AI

- Confirm originality and any relationship to thesis, preprint, conference, or prior article.
- Interpret iThenticate overlap by source and attribution; do not use an invented universal percentage.
- Complete the Elsevier competing-interest declarations tool.
- Verify authorship approval and CRediT roles.
- Check dataset permission, biometric privacy, figure rights, and source-code licenses.
- If AI made substantive changes to organization/content, include the current Elsevier AI declaration before references.
- If AI was part of research methods, describe it reproducibly in Methods.
- If AI assisted an explanatory figure, disclose tool/use in its caption and the general declaration.
- Reject fabricated/altered research images or data visualizations not derived reproducibly from data.
- Do not use general-purpose generative AI for a graphical abstract.

## Submission-candidate gate

Do not assign `submission candidate` until:

- no fatal or major finding remains;
- every central result is final and traceable to an artifact;
- every major claim has protocol-compatible evidence;
- all placeholders and contradictory values are removed;
- the complete submission package has been checked against the current official guide;
- all authors have reviewed the final text and declarations.
