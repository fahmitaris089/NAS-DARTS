## Positioning and editorial process

IJCCE seeks cognitive-computing methods applied to real engineering problems, including embedded data technologies and AI-supported decision systems. Explain the engineering problem, the cognitive/learning contribution, and the practical evaluation; a generic classifier comparison is insufficient positioning.

The journal uses single-blind peer review. The editor first checks suitability and may desk-reject work for scope mismatch, ethical problems, high text similarity, insufficient originality, or flaws in design and methods. Suitable manuscripts are normally evaluated by at least two independent reviewers.

Do not promise acceptance or infer acceptance probability from quartile, CiteScore, or formatting compliance.

## Source format and structure

- Submit the native word-processor source. Use a simple single-column layout.
- Do not justify text or enable automatic hyphenation.
- Word and LaTeX are accepted; Elsevier `elsarticle` is preferred when using LaTeX.
- Number sections and subsections; use their numbers for internal cross-reference.
- Introduction: state objectives and sufficient background without a detailed survey or results summary.
- Methods: provide enough detail for independent reproduction and describe modifications to published methods.
- Results: clear and concise.
- Discussion: interpret significance rather than repeat results; combined Results and Discussion is acceptable.
- Conclusions: short and bounded by the study.

The public guide does not state a full-article page limit, word limit, fixed similarity percentage, or AI-detector threshold. Do not invent one from published article length.

## Front and end matter

- Title: concise, informative, and preferably free of abbreviations/formulae.
- Provide complete author names, affiliations, countries, and corresponding-author contact.
- Abstract: concise, factual, stand-alone, and normally without references.
- Keywords: include terms associated with the paper.
- Define nonstandard abbreviations at first use and keep them consistent.
- Place acknowledgements before references, not on the title page.
- Provide CRediT roles for each contributor.
- State funding and the funder's role.
- Complete the Elsevier declarations tool and upload the resulting competing-interest DOCX.
- Add a data/code availability statement appropriate to licenses, privacy, and actual access.
- Add an AI-use declaration immediately before references when required by the current Elsevier policy.

Highlights are optional but strongly encouraged. Upload them as a separate editable file containing 3–5 bullets, each no longer than 85 characters including spaces.

A graphical abstract is optional. If supplied, follow current size, file-type, licensing, and AI-tool rules. Do not treat it as an ordinary figure.

## Equations, figures, and tables

- Equations must remain editable; italicize variables and number displayed equations that are cited.
- Place figure captions outside the image. Give a brief title and sufficient explanation of symbols and abbreviations.
- Upload each final illustration separately with logical filenames and uniform lettering.
- Preferred figure fonts include Arial, Courier, Times New Roman, and Symbol.
- Minimum raster resolution: 300 dpi for photographs, 1000 dpi for line drawings, and 500 dpi for combination artwork.
- Tables must be editable, numbered consecutively, and referenced in the text.
- Put table notes below the body. Avoid vertical rules and cell shading.
- Do not duplicate the same results in prose, table, and figure without a clear purpose.
- Turn off Track Changes before submission and supplementary-file delivery.

## References

- Use APA author–year citations in text.
- Arrange references alphabetically and then chronologically where needed.
- Ensure every in-text citation has a reference entry and every reference entry is cited.
- Prefer the peer-reviewed publication when a cited preprint has been formally published.
- Mark central preprints explicitly and include the preprint DOI where available.
- For web references, include a full URL and access date at minimum.
- Verify source metadata from the publication, publisher, DOI resolver, or another authoritative index.

## Submission package check

- Native manuscript source with complete title page.
- Keywords, all editable tables, and all captions.
- Separate high-resolution artwork files.
- Optional separate highlights and graphical abstract.
- Competing-interest declaration DOCX.
- CRediT, funding, acknowledgement, data/code availability, and AI declaration as applicable.
- Permissions for third-party copyrighted material.
- Consistent grammar, reference style, citation reciprocity, and no unresolved placeholders.
